# TBIPTV Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

TBIPTV ("the app") is developed by Langeydong as a personal, non-commercial project. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## What the app is

TBIPTV is a media player. It does not include, sell, host or link to any channels, films, series or subscriptions. To use it you add a playlist (an Xstream Codes login or an M3U address) from a service you are legally entitled to watch.

## Information stored on your device

- The playlist addresses, usernames and passwords you enter.
- Your favourites, watch history and resume positions.
- Cached film, series and cast information.

All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to the developer. You can delete a saved playlist with **Settings → Disconnect Playlist**, and uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

## Information sent to other services

- **Your playlist provider.** Your login details are sent only to the server address you enter, so that the app can load your playlist and play streams. That provider's own privacy policy applies.
- **Wikipedia and Wikidata (Wikimedia Foundation).** To show film and series summaries, cast photos and biographies, the app sends the title of the film or series, or the name of the person, to Wikipedia and Wikidata. No login details or personal information are sent. See https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy

## What the app does not do

- No user accounts, sign-up or tracking.
- No advertising.
- No analytics or crash reporting sent to the developer.
- No sale or sharing of personal information.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
