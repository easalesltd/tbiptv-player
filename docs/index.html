What you get

Live TV

Channels in categories, plus a TV guide if your playlist has one. You can hide anything that isn’t marked FHD, watch something again when the playlist keeps it, and get a reminder shortly before a programme starts.

Films and series

Posters, plot, cast and episodes. It remembers where you stopped and offers the next episode. If the playlist has a trailer, it plays in the app.

Sport

What’s on, grouped by sport, country and league from the channel name and the guide. A match starting within two hours still counts as on now.

Mini player

Keep watching in the corner while you browse. On films and catch-up you can change speed, soundtrack, subtitles and how the picture fits.

Favourites and search

Save channels to folders, hide or PIN-lock categories, and search even if a letter is wrong. Search can find an actor too.

Stays on your device

Your login only goes to the server you typed in. There is no TBIPTV account, no ads and no tracking.
