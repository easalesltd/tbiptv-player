TBIPTV

A player for the playlist you already have

TBIPTV plays your own Xtream or M3U login on Xbox or Mac. It comes with no channels, and you pay £2.99 once, with no subscription.

Xbox: Microsoft Store Mac: buy and download

How it works

1

Install it.

2

Enter the server, username and password from your provider, or paste an M3U link.

3

Pick something and press play.

A friend told me there were no good IPTV apps for Xbox or Mac, so I made them.

What it does

Live TV

Categories and a guide, catch-up where your playlist supports it, and reminders before a programme starts. You can hide anything that isn’t FHD.

Films and series

Posters, cast and plot, resume where you stopped, and the next episode offered. Trailers play in-app if the playlist has one.

Sport

Channels grouped by sport, country and league, taken from the channel name and guide. A match starting within two hours counts as on now.

Everything else

A mini player while you browse (with speed, soundtrack, subtitles and picture fit on films and catch-up), PIN-locked or hidden categories, favourite folders, and typo-tolerant search that finds actors too.

Privacy

Your login goes only to the server you entered. No TBIPTV account, no ads, no tracking.

FAQ

Does it include channels?

No. You need a playlist from a service you’re allowed to use.

Mac says it can’t verify the app.

Drag the app into Applications. Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 14 or later.
