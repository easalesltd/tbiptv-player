TBIPTV Get it on Xbox

A television player for playlists you already have.

TBIPTV plays your own Xtream Codes login or M3U playlist on Xbox, with a layout made for the controller and a big screen.

Get TBIPTV How to start

On your Xbox, open the Microsoft Store and search for TBIPTV.

A player only. TBIPTV does not include, sell or link to any channels, films, series or subscriptions. You add a playlist from a service you are allowed to watch.

How to start

1
Install it from the Store

Search for TBIPTV on your Xbox, or use the button on this page from a phone or computer.

2
Add your playlist

Type the server address, username and password from your provider, or paste the full link they sent you. An M3U address works too.

3
Watch with the controller

Live TV, a guide, films and series. Favourites, resume, and a small player in the corner while you browse.

What it does

Live TV

Categories, channel names and a TV guide when your playlist includes one.

Films and series

Posters, plots, cast and episode lists, with resume and the next episode.

Mini player

Keep the picture in the corner while you look through the library.

Private on your console

Logins stay on the Xbox and are sent only to the server you enter. No account, adverts or tracking.
