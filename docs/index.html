What it does

In the car

CarPlay and Android Auto

On iPhone it appears in CarPlay. On Android it appears in Android Auto. Browse Home, channels, films and series from the car, search, and listen in Now Playing. CarPlay shows posters. Search with the in-car keyboard, or say a name to Siri. The car plays the sound. It does not include channels.

Live TV

Categories and a guide, catch-up where your playlist supports it, and reminders before a programme starts. You can hide anything that isn’t FHD. Channel names keep FHD, HD or SD on the end, so two copies of the same channel stay distinct.

Films and series

Posters, cast and plot, resume where you stopped, and the next episode offered. On a film or episode, a quick press left or right jumps 10 seconds. Hold to move further along, then let go and it plays from there. A film or episode kept as MKV plays when the device can decode the picture, and surround sound plays in stereo. Home can list films and series that are popular right now, but only if they are already in your playlist. Playlist names can drop extra tags such as 4K. Trailers play in-app if the playlist has one.

Sport

Channels grouped by sport, country and league, taken from the channel name and guide. A match starting within two hours counts as on now.

More in the app

Picture in Picture

On iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Android phones and tablets, leave the app and keep watching in a Picture in Picture window. On Google TV, Xbox and Windows, a mini player sits in the corner while you browse. On a computer, a double-click tucks the picture into the corner.

Mini player

Keep watching in the corner while you browse. On a computer, a double-click tucks the picture into the corner, and the mouse pointer stays visible. On films and catch-up you can change speed, soundtrack, subtitles and how the picture fits.

Search

While a stream plays you can search channels, jump back through recent channels. Typo-tolerant search that finds actors too.

Lock controls

Tap lock on the player bar so taps on the remote or touchscreen do not skip or pause by mistake. Unlock when you want the controls back.

Stream info

While something plays, open stream info to see the address, format and quality hints the player is using. Handy when a channel fails or looks soft.

PIN-locked categories

Set a parental PIN and lock or hide categories by name. Adults stay behind the PIN until it is entered.

Favourites

Favourites are a list of names, so you can read them, and you can group them in folders.

TV guide (EPG)

See what is on now and next across live channels. The guide uses your playlist’s XMLTV data. Set a reminder before a show starts, and open catch-up when the channel supports it.

FAQ

Does it include channels?

No. You need a playlist from a service you’re allowed to use.

Does it support Xtream Codes?

Yes. Enter the server, username and password from your provider, or paste an M3U link. TBIPTV Xstream is a player only.

Does it have Picture in Picture?

Yes on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Android phones and tablets. Leave the app and playback can continue in a Picture in Picture window. On Google TV, Xbox and Windows, a mini player sits in the corner while you browse. Picture in Picture is not on Apple TV.

Does it work with CarPlay and Android Auto?

Yes. On iPhone it appears in CarPlay. On Android it appears in Android Auto. Browse Home, channels, films and series, search, and listen from Now Playing. CarPlay shows posters. Search with the in-car keyboard, or say a name to Siri. The car plays the sound. It does not include channels.

Is there a monthly subscription?

No. The app is £2.99 once. There is no monthly TBIPTV subscription. Your playlist still comes from a service you already use.

Does the Mac app work on Apple silicon and Intel?

Yes. TBIPTV Xstream for Mac runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 14 or later. Get it from the App Store.

Which devices and OS versions are supported?

iPhone and iPad need iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. Mac needs macOS 14 or later. Apple TV needs tvOS 17 or later. Xbox and Windows are on the Microsoft Store. Android phone and tablet are on Google Play (Android 8 or later). Google TV is on the way.

What are Xtream Codes?

Many IPTV services give you a server address, username and password—that login style is often called Xtream Codes. TBIPTV Xstream uses it to load your playlist. It does not sell a service or channels.

Do Xtream and M3U work on every platform?

Yes on every released app. Use Xtream server, username and password, or paste an M3U URL. The app only connects to the server you enter.

Why does Mac use the same App Store link as iPhone?

Apple lists TBIPTV Xstream as one app. One purchase on your Apple ID can cover iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

How do refunds and support work?

Refunds are through Apple or Microsoft under their store rules. There is no TBIPTV account. Bugs and ideas: use Questions and support below or GitHub.
