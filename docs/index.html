TBIPTV Get it on Xbox

Your own IPTV playlist, on your Xbox

TBIPTV means The Best IPTV. It is a simple player for an Xtream Codes login, also written Xstream, or an M3U playlist. It's built for a controller and a TV, so you're not squinting at a tiny phone-style menu from the sofa.

£2.99 once. No monthly subscription.

Get TBIPTV How to start

TBIPTV is listed in the Microsoft Store. On an Xbox, open the Store and search for TBIPTV.

One thing to be clear about. TBIPTV is only a player. It doesn't come with any channels, films, series or subscriptions, and it doesn't sell or link to any. You bring a playlist from a service you're legitimately allowed to watch.

Getting started

1
Install it

Search for TBIPTV in the Store on your Xbox. If you're on a phone or computer, the button above takes you straight to the listing.

2
Add your playlist

Enter the server address, username and password your provider gave you, or paste the full link they sent. An M3U address works too.

3
Pick up the controller

That's it. Browse, pick something and watch.

What you get

Live TV

Channels sorted into categories, with a TV guide if your playlist includes one. An FHD switch hides channels whose names do not say FHD. Finished programmes can be watched again when the playlist keeps them. A short guide stays on screen while you watch, and a reminder pops up shortly before something starts, as long as TBIPTV is open.

Films and series

Posters, plot summaries, cast and episode lists. It remembers where you stopped and lines up the next episode. A trailer plays in the app when the playlist includes one.

Sport

What is on is grouped by sport, then by country and league, using the channel name and the TV guide. Football stays with football. A game starting within two hours still counts as on now, and you can hide channels with nothing listed.

Mini player

Carry on watching in the corner of the screen while you browse for what's next. On films and catch-up you can change the speed, pick another soundtrack or subtitles when the stream has them, and choose how the picture fits the screen.

Yours to arrange

On Live TV, highlight a channel and press Add to Favourites. The same button is on the TV guide, and on screen while that channel is playing. Favourites can be filed into folders. Hide categories you do not want to see, or lock others behind a PIN. Search still finds a title if one letter is wrong, and it can find an actor.

Private by design

Your login stays on your Xbox and only goes to the server you typed in. The channel list is kept on the console, so the next time you open the app it comes up faster. There's no account to create, no adverts and no tracking.
