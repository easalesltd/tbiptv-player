TBIPTV

Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

TBIPTV is a media player developed by Langeydong. This page explains what the app does with your information on Xbox and Mac.

What the app is

TBIPTV does not include, sell, host or link to any channels, films, series or subscriptions. You add a playlist (an Xtream Codes login or an M3U address) from a service you are legally entitled to watch.

Information stored on your device

  • The playlist addresses, usernames and passwords you enter.
  • Your favourites, watch history, resume positions and downloads.
  • Cached film, series and cast information.

All of this stays in the app’s private storage on your device. It is never sent to the developer. You can delete a saved playlist in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes its data.

Information sent to other services

  • Your playlist provider. Your login details are sent only to the server address you enter, so the app can load your playlist and play streams. That provider’s own privacy policy applies.
  • Wikipedia and Wikidata (Wikimedia Foundation). To show film and series summaries, cast photos and biographies, the app sends the title of the film or series, or the name of the person. Home also reads public Wikipedia page-view charts and keeps only names that already appear in your playlist. No login details are sent.
  • Apple media charts. To show trending films and series on Home, the app reads Apple’s public UK charts and keeps only titles that already appear in your playlist. No login details are sent.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-up or tracking.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or crash reporting sent to the developer.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
